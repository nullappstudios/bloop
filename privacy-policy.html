Bloop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Bloop is a fully offline puzzle game. It does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, best times, Daily streak, Jelly Rush best score, unlocked Bloops) and your settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): used only to show the daily reminder that you switch on yourself. The reminder is scheduled and displayed entirely on your device. You can turn it off in the app's Settings or in Android settings at any time.

What Bloop does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No internet access, analytics, crash-reporting service, advertising SDKs or trackers.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Bloop contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat and no external links, so it is suitable for general audiences.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if online leaderboards are added), the updated policy will be published on this page and in the store listing before the change ships.
